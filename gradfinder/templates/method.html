What the numbers mean

Read this before comparing two states.

Which metric to trust

The map draws one of two numbers. Institutions counts institutions in scope and is comparable between states: enumerating the doctoral institutions in a state is a bounded job and these rosters are believed complete. Programs on file is not comparable between states — it measures how deeply each pack was typed. Compare states on institutions; use programs to look inside one state.

Faculty lens

A separate layer built top-down: papers traced from the FORGE phonon seed (Chen et al., Adv. Sci. 2021) to their senior authors, then to those authors' current universities. Map colour is the count of faculty with fit 4 or 5; fit is a hand judgment. Arizona was swept department by department and every other state only through papers, so these counts are not comparable between states — compare on best fit. Details in docs/TOPDOWN.md.

Counting rule

A number in this console is a count of programs recorded in the state's data pack. It is not a count of programs offered. Until a state's pack has been rebuilt from IPEDS Completions, the two differ, and the gap is not uniform between institutions.

Scope

Research doctorates only. Professional practice doctorates — MD, DO, PharmD, DNP, DPT, OTD, DBA, EdD, PsyD — are out of scope even where the field is technical.

The last two families are boundary cases. Psychology sits inside NSF's STEM definition and outside several others; biomedical and health research splits on whether the plan is a research doctorate or a clinical one. Both are tagged so they can be included or dropped, rather than silently folded in.

Layers

The pipeline runs L0 roster → L1 eligible → L2 sourced → L3 resolved → L4 dossier. Every pack declares which layer it has reached. Arizona is at L1-seed: institutions enumerated by hand, programs representative, no IPEDS join, no funding or deadline fields.

Known weaknesses

  • Program lists for the two large public universities are representative, not exhaustive, so their totals are floors.
  • ASU expresses many plans as school-level doctorates with named concentrations; a concentration list and a plan list give different counts.
  • Multi-campus institutions get one marker, at the flagship campus.
  • Program counts across states compare collection effort, not program supply. The California lists are shallower relative to institution size than the Arizona ones.
  • Program links run a site search on the institution's domain. The packs carry no verified program URLs, and constructing plausible ones would produce authoritative-looking 404s.
  • Nothing here is scraped or machine-verified. Every record was typed by hand.
